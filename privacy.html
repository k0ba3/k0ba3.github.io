Assignments Widget - Privacy Policy

This application is for the personal use of its owner only. It accesses the owner's own Google Calendar data (read-only) via the Google Calendar API solely to display upcoming assignment due dates on the owner's personal computer.

No data is transmitted to, stored on, or shared with any third party or external server. All data is processed and stored only on the owner's own device.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
